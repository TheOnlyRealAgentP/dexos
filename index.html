Dex OS is a complete operating system for your PC, built on Fedora Linux, with its own desktop and apps. Install it in place of Windows. It looks good out of the box, updates without breaking, and still opens the Windows programs and Android apps you rely on.

Tour

Everything has a place.

No settings to hunt for and no terminal needed. The things you use every day sit on a home screen made for a big display.

A home screen for your desktop

Pages of apps and widgets: calendar, reminders, weather, device care, your media and Dexter, the built-in assistant. Rearrange them, add pages, or search apps, files and the web from the bar at the top.

  • Taskbar with favourites, recent apps and open windows
  • Four desktops, an overview of every window, and snap layouts
  • Touchpad gestures that follow your fingers

Two dozen apps, already there

Files, Settings, Notes, Gallery, Music, Calendar, Clock, Weather, Email, Contacts, Messages, a voice recorder, a screen recorder and more. They're made for Dex OS, so they all look and work the same way.

Messages sends texts through your phone, and Gallery can clean up old photos.

One click to the switches you use

Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, dark mode, eye comfort, power mode, VPN, screen recording and casting to a TV all live in the Quick panel. Notifications get their own panel. Both float over a frosted-glass blur.

Make it yours

94 wallpapers come built in. They're drawn as vector art, so they stay sharp on any screen. Pick a colour palette and the taskbar, panels, lock screen and apps all follow it.

Runs your apps

Leaving Windows shouldn't mean leaving your apps.

Double-click an installer like you always have. Dex OS works out what it is and how to run it.

Windows programs

Double-click an .exe or .msi and it installs with its own icon in your apps, through the same compatibility layer Steam uses for games. For the few programs that won't run that way, like ArcGIS Pro or Autodesk tools, Dex OS can set up a Windows virtual machine whose apps open as normal windows. That needs your own Windows license.

Android apps

Double-click an .apk. Simple apps run directly; the rest run in an Android system that stays out of sight. Either way, each app gets its own icon and window.

Dex Store

Thousands of Linux apps from Flathub and the Snap Store in one place, with charts, categories and editor's picks.

Gaming

Ready to play on day one.

Steam is installed, and so is NVIDIA's driver for GeForce RTX and GTX 16 series cards. AMD and Intel graphics work out of the box. Many of the gaming tweaks from Nobara are already applied.

Game Hub keeps one set of settings for all your games and lets you change any of them for a single game: power mode, frame rate cap, V-Sync, low latency, FSR upscaling and sharpening. Press Super+G for the performance overlay.

Built not to break

An update should never cost you a weekend.

The core of Dex OS is read-only and checked every time it starts. Everything else, your files, settings and installed programs, stays fully yours.

Updates in the background

They download while you work, skip metered connections, and install when you restart, all at once or not at all.

Undone if they fail

After an update, Dex OS checks that the login screen and desktop come up. If they don't, it goes back on its own.

One part at a time

The desktop, Settings, Files and other parts update separately, so a fix for one doesn't wait for the rest.

Safe mode

The boot menu always has a way back to the version that shipped, and Settings can roll back to the last one.

Private and secure

Your stuff stays yours.

Disk encryption

Unlocks with the PC's security chip, with a recovery key for backup.

Fingerprint sign-in

On laptops with a reader.

Secure Folder

An encrypted folder for the files that matter most.

VPN with a kill switch

WireGuard and OpenVPN, per-app VPN, and auto-connect on public Wi-Fi.

Auto Blocker

USB devices plugged in while the PC is locked stay blocked.

Passwords and passkeys

A password manager with browser autofill comes installed.

And more

The small things add up.

Your cloud drives in Files

Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, Box, Nextcloud and network shares, downloaded only when you open a file.

Phone link

Texts, notifications and phone mirroring.

Smart select

Grab part of the screen and copy the text in it.

Clipboard history

Text, pictures and files you copied earlier.

Multiple displays

Every screen at its sharpest resolution and fastest refresh rate. Super+P switches layouts.

File history

Hourly snapshots of your home folder.

Video that plays

H.264 and HEVC, with hardware decoding where your GPU has it.

Made for developers too

Install tools the normal way, no containers required. Claude, Gemini and Codex CLIs get their keys from the security chip.

Accessible from the first screen

The screen reader is available before you've set anything up.

Get Dex OS

Try it before you install it.

  1. 1
    Write it to a USB stick. Use Fedora Media Writer, balenaEtcher or Rufus in DD mode, on a stick of 16 GB or more.
  2. 2
    Start your PC from the stick and pick "Try without installing." Nothing on your PC changes.
  3. 3
    Like it? Install it in about 10 minutes, in place of Windows. Not ready to let go? The installer can also put it next to Windows.
Download coming soon

Dex OS 0.1 is an early preview. It's tested in virtual machines, and testing on real PCs is under way. The first public download will be posted here.

What you need

  • A 64-bit PC with UEFI firmware
  • A USB stick of 16 GB or more
  • NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics. Older GeForce cards (GTX 900 and 10 series) use the open driver and run games slower.
  • Secure Boot turned off for now. Signed builds are planned.
